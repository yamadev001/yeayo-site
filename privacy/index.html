Yeayo privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Yeayo is a small app for private, invite-only weight-loss groups. It is run by Yama Mohmad ("we"). This policy explains what the app keeps about you, who can see it, and how to remove it. If you have a question, email yeayo1@pm.me.

The short version

  • We keep only what the app needs to work: your email, the weights you enter, what you post in your groups, and, only if you turn it on, figures from your phone's Health app (Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android): your workouts, steps, distance and energy. If you choose, you can also add photos and write private notes to yourself.
  • Only members of your groups see your group activity, and the group's owner decides whether members see actual weights or only how much was lost.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

What we keep

Your account. Your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash, so nobody, including us, can read it. We also keep your time zone, so weekly cutoffs and reminders happen at the right local time, and the unit you prefer to type weights in.

Your weigh-ins. The weights you enter, the unit you entered them in, and when. Your start weight and goal weight, if set. If an owner or coach corrects one of your weigh-ins, we keep a record of the change: who made it, when, the old and new value, and the reason they gave.

Your groups. The name you use in each group, your role (owner, coach or athlete), whether you are taking part in the weekly results, and when you joined or left. We keep a log of changes to group settings and membership, such as role changes, so the group can see what happened. We also record when someone tries to join with an invite code, so repeated wrong guesses can be slowed down.

What you post. Reactions and comments on weekly results, and any images an owner or coach uploads, such as a screenshot of the group's Strava leaderboard.

Your group's conversation. The messages you send to a group (up to 1,000 characters each), with the time, whether you edited one, and which message it replies to; the reactions you give to messages, weigh-ins and weekly results (the group can see who gave which emoji); and polls you create (a question and its options) and the votes you cast (a vote is shown with your name). You can edit or delete your own messages; a group's owner or coach can delete anyone's. A deleted message's words are removed, and its replies show "Deleted message". Messages are seen only by the group's current members.

Photos in the conversation, only if you send one. A photo sent with a message is shrunk on your phone to a JPEG of at most 1,600 pixels (2 MB at most) and kept in private storage, together with its size and whether it has a caption. Only the group's current members can see it, through a link that expires after an hour. You can delete your own photo, and a group's owner or coach can delete anyone's; the file goes with the message. Tapping "Save photo" in the app saves a copy to your own phone; whoever has the photo on their phone can keep it, like any picture sent to them.

Notifications. Your notification settings, and, if you allow notifications, a push token for your phone. The token is an address that lets us send a notification to your phone; it does not identify you to anyone else. We keep a record of each notification sent to you: its kind, its general text, such as the group's name and "Sam commented on your week", and whether it arrived. Notifications never contain a weight or the words of a comment. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself.

Photos, only if you add them. A profile photo, and for a group's owner or coach a group photo and a cover photo. We keep each as a small picture (and a larger copy for viewing full screen) in private storage. They are shown only to people who share a group with you (a group's photos, to its members).

A weigh-in saved with no signal. If you weigh in with no connection, the weight stays on your phone until it can be sent, then is sent like any weigh-in.

Time away, only if you set it. If you say you are away (up to three weeks), we keep the dates (from when, until which day), never a reason. In each of your groups that has a coach, the coach (or the owner, if there is none) is shown your request with the dates and approves or declines it; until they approve, you are not away in that group. We keep one request for each such group: the dates, whether it is waiting, approved or declined, and when and by whom it was answered. Your groups see "away until 3 Dec" once it applies, and nothing else. Your pause and your requests are removed with your account.

A line for your week, only if you post one. It is posted as a message in your group's conversation, like any message, and is removed with your account. When everyone in a group weighs in for several weeks running, the group sees that as a team streak, with no names or weights, and members can cheer it; your cheer is removed with your account.

Captain of the week, only if the owner turns it on. The one-line theme a captain sets for the week and the Captain's Pick (who it went to and the words). The whole group sees them in the conversation. If you were captain, they are deleted with your account.

Your reason, only if you write it. One line about why you are doing this. It is private to you unless you choose "Show in my groups", and then the members of your groups see it on your progress and in the Members list. It never appears on a leaderboard or in a notification.

Weight history, only if it is loaded for you. Weigh-ins from before you used the app (for example from a spreadsheet or a Garmin export), with the date, the time if there is one, and a note if you wrote one. It is loaded by the app's owner at your request. It is private to you unless it was loaded to show in a group, and then that group's members see it on the group's charts under the group's weight settings. It never creates a message, a notification, a milestone or a rank.

Private notes to yourself, only if you write them. A mood you can tag on a weigh-in (great, good, okay, tired or stressed), a one-line note you can add to a weigh-in, and a letter to your future self. Only you can read them; no group, coach or owner can. The app uses your mood tags to show you, on your own page, how your weight moved in the weeks you chose each mood.

Feedback and error reports. If you send feedback from Settings, we keep your note and the app version, and only the person who makes Yeayo reads it. We also email your note, with the name you use in your first group and the app version (never your email address), to the person who makes Yeayo, through Resend. When the app hits an error in its own code, it sends the error message, a technical trace, the app version and the type of phone (iPhone or Android) to our own database, so the error can be fixed. No outside crash-reporting or analytics service gets anything, and neither contains your weights. We also keep, for each group, the time you last had its conversation open, to show how many messages are new.

Asking your coach for support, only if you use it. If you tap "Send private message to coach" in a group, we keep that request, and the short note you may add, for a week or until you clear it. Only the group's coach (or its owner, if it has no coach) can see it, and can send you a short private reply that only you see.

On your phone. The app stores your sign-in session, an invite code you opened before signing in, and whether it has already asked about notifications.

Health data from your phone, only if you turn it on. In Settings, "Import Health data from phone" is off until you turn it on. Only then, and only after your phone asks you for permission (Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android), Yeayo reads from Health: your workouts (when each started, how long it lasted, what kind of workout it was, such as running or yoga, and how far you went and how many metres you climbed if Health recorded them), and your daily steps, distance and active energy. It reads nothing else (not heart rate, sleep, location or any other health data). Each time the app opens, it sends us the figures for each of the last six months of days (about 183), as daily totals by kind of workout and as daily totals of steps, distance and energy. On Android, Health Connect may let Yeayo see only the last 30 days; then only those days are sent. We keep those daily figures. Turning "Import Health data from phone" off in Settings deletes everything it sent, at once.

Who sees your Health figures. Imported figures are visible only to you until you turn on "Visible to others". Then the groups you are in see only the types you switch on in the list: Activities (the kinds of workout), Workout hours, Distance, Steps, Elevation and Energy. A type you leave off stays private to you, and is never sent to a group, not even as zero.

Your weight from Health, only when you ask. On the weigh-in screen, if you tap "Use a weight from Apple Health" (on Android, "Use a weight from Health Connect") and your phone asks for permission and you allow it, Yeayo reads your body weight readings from the last two weeks on your phone and lists them, with the date and time each was taken, so you can pick one for your weigh-in. The weights themselves are not sent to us. We are only asked when each reading was taken, to check that it was taken inside the weigh-in window. A weight reaches us only if you press Submit, as a normal weigh-in.

Weight, workouts, steps and energy are health-related information. Yeayo is not a medical app and does not give medical advice. Apart from what you turn on or ask for above, it does not read from Apple Health, Health Connect or any other health service. We use Health data only to show you your own figures and, if you choose, your groups' figures in the app. We never use it for advertising or marketing, never sell it, and never transfer it to anyone other than the service providers listed below, who handle it only to run the app.

Who can see it

Members of your group. Members see your group name, your role and your progress in that group. Whether they see your actual weights or only the weight you lost depends on the group's setting. If an owner switches a group to show actual weights, you are asked whether to reveal weights you entered before the switch, and they stay hidden until you answer. Only you see the old value of a corrected weigh-in. Members never see your email address.

Your Health figures, if you make them visible. Members of each group you are in see, for each week, your totals for only the types you switched on: for example your workout hours (such as "3.5 h") and the kinds of workout you did (such as "Running 2.0 h"), your distance, your steps, your metres climbed or your energy. If a group's owner or coach sets a weekly target in hours, kilometres or metres climbed, members also see your total for the week and overall in that measure, against the target. They never see the individual workouts or the days. If the group travels a virtual route together, the group sees one total of kilometres for everyone who shares, never yours alone.

Only you. Your mood tags, your weigh-in notes and your letter are seen by you alone. A request for support is seen only by your group's coach, or its owner if it has no coach, and the reply only by you. A group's owner and coaches also see a short "needs attention" list for the group: who has missed two or more weeks in a row, whose weight has not come down over their last four weigh-ins, and who has no goal. It shows no weights, and nobody else sees it.

Nobody outside your groups. Groups are private and joined only by invite. Other app users cannot see your groups or anything in them.

Us. As the people who run the app, we can access the stored data to keep the service working, fix problems and answer your requests. We do not look at it for any other purpose.

Service providers who run parts of the app for us, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase stores the database, accounts and images, and sends sign-in emails through Resend. Data is stored in the United States (Oregon).
  • Expo delivers push notifications, through Apple's push service on iPhone and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. A notification contains only its title, its general text and which screen to open.
  • Apple and Google distribute the app through their stores, under their own privacy policies.

These providers may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to run and protect their services.

Strava. A group can save a link to its Strava club. Yeayo does not connect to your Strava account or read any Strava data. Opening the link takes you to Strava, under Strava's own privacy policy.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account.

  • Leaving a group. If you leave or are removed from a group, your history in that group is kept, in case you come back, but you no longer appear in its lists or results. Your comments stay visible, with your name greyed out. Your weigh-ins stay yours: the app offers to keep a copy of them, with your goal and starting weight, in a new group of your own where you are the only member until you invite someone. The copy is made only when you tap the button, and the old group's copy stays as it was. You can also download everything with Download my data.
  • Health data. Turning "Import Health data from phone" off in Settings deletes everything it sent, at once. Turning "Visible to others" or a single type off stops your groups seeing it straight away. You can also stop Yeayo reading your Health data in your phone: in Apple's Health app under Sharing, Apps, or in Android's Health Connect under App permissions.
  • Photos. You can delete a photo you sent in the conversation (the file goes with it); you can remove your profile photo in Settings, and a group's owner or coach can remove the group's photos in its settings; removing a photo deletes the files. A new photo replaces the old one and deletes it.
  • Your letter and mood tags. You can change or throw away your letter before it comes back to you, and throw it away after. Mood tags and weigh-in notes are deleted with your account. A request for support clears itself after a week.
  • Deleting a group. When an owner deletes a group, everything in it is deleted: weigh-ins, comments, reactions and images.
  • Deleting your account. You can delete your account in the app's Settings. This deletes your account, your weigh-ins, your imported Health figures, your mood tags, your weigh-in notes, your letter, your reason, your weight history, your captain themes and picks, your feedback notes, your error reports, your last-read markers, your requests for support, your pause dates and requests for time away, your cheers, your group memberships, your comments and reactions, your messages, your poll votes and the polls you created, your notification settings and your push tokens. Photos you sent in a conversation are deleted with your messages; if a file cannot be removed at once, it stays in private storage where no member of the app can open it, until we remove it. Images you uploaded for a group (its photo and leaderboard screenshots) stay with the group, because they belong to the group, but they are no longer linked to you. Records of changes you made to a group, such as a correction, stay in the group's log without your name. If you own a group that still has other members, you first hand it to someone else or delete it.

Notifications that could not be sent within six hours are not sent at all. Records of notifications are deleted with your account, or with the group they were about.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers. Who can see what is enforced by the database itself, not only by the app, so a member cannot read another group's data even with a modified app. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your data.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your weigh-ins, names and notification settings in the app.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Turn Health import off in Settings, which deletes what was sent, or switch off what your groups can see. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone outside your groups.
  • Download your own data in Settings: your weigh-ins (with the group name), workout hours, mood tags, weigh-in notes, letter, reason, weight history and requests for support, as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full JSON file. The file is made on your phone and shared through your phone's own share sheet; we do not store a copy. Photos and messages are not in it yet.
  • Delete your account in Settings, or ask us at yeayo1@pm.me to delete it or to send you a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as objecting to how your data is used or complaining to a data protection authority. Email us and we will help.

Age

Yeayo is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
