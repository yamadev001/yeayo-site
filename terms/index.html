What Yeayo is

A private, invite-only app for weekly weigh-ins and encouragement in a small group. It is for adults (18+). It is fitness and accountability, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your diet or exercise.

Your account

Keep your password safe and give true details. You choose what the group sees. You can delete your account at any time.

Be kind

  • No harassment, shaming of bodies, hate, spam or anything illegal.
  • Do not share other people's private information or photos without their say-so.
  • Group owners and coaches can remove messages and members. We may remove content or accounts that break these rules.

Reports and blocks

You can report any message and block any member. Reports go to the group's owner and coaches.

Your content

What you post stays yours. You let Yeayo store it and show it to your group so the app can work.
