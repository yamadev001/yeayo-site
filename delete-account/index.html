Yeayo

Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Yeayo and go to You, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account, weigh-ins, messages, photos and Health-connected data are deleted. Nothing is kept for later.

Without the app

Write to yeayo1@pm.me from the email address you signed up with and ask for your account to be deleted. We confirm by reply.

Before you go

If you own a group that still has other members, hand it to another member first; the app will not delete the account until you do. You can also ask for a copy of your data at the same address.
